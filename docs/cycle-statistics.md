# Cycle Statistics & Metrics

Computational rules for member dashboards and cycle summary statistics.

## Metrics
- **Collection Rate**: Percentage of members who have cleared cycle installments.
- **Average Discount**: Mean bidding discount across completed cycles.
- **Cumulative Dividend**: Total savings earned per member over group lifecycle.

### Yield Calculation
Annualized percentage yield (APY) takes into account compound discounts across 12-month periods.
